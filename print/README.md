# Print files

Four posters, generated from `poster-template.html` by `py make_poster.py`
and printed to PDF from Chrome. Do not edit these by hand; regenerate them.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `MushroomsKingdom-poster-A4-press.pdf` | A print shop. Dark, full-bleed photograph |
| `MushroomsKingdom-poster-A4-home.pdf` | An office or home printer. Light, photo reduced to a band |
| `MushroomsKingdom-poster-A5-press.pdf` | As above, A5 |
| `MushroomsKingdom-poster-A5-home.pdf` | As above, A5 |

**Press or home is an ink decision, not a size one.** The press sheet covers
the page in a dark photograph: excellent from a real printer, poor from an
inkjet, where large flat dark areas band and the white QR panel smears at its
edges. The QR is the one element that must stay sharp enough to scan.

No bleed and no crop marks. The sheet has square corners and the print shop
handles trim.

All four are a single page at the correct paper size, verified against the
PDF itself rather than a preview. The QR encodes
`https://mushroomkingdom.co.il/catalogue/`; if that address ever changes,
every printed copy stops working.
