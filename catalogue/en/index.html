CATALOGUE 2026

Lion’s Mane

פטריית רעמת האריה

Hericium erinaceus

“Nature’s steak” - a rich, meaty texture. Regarded as a superfood that supports brain health and improves focus and concentration.

Pink Oyster

פטריית צדף ורודה

Pleurotus djamor

A strikingly beautiful mushroom in a vivid colour, rich in iron, amino acids and antioxidants. Works beautifully in baked dishes, pasta and stir-fries.

Blue Oyster

פטריית צדף כחולה

Pleurotus ostreatus var. columbinus

A succulent mushroom with a delicate earthy flavour that works beautifully roasted or seared. A natural source of protein and dietary fibre, packed with antioxidants that support the immune system and help reduce inflammation.

Golden Oyster

פטריית צדף צהובה

Pleurotus citrinopileatus

An exotic mushroom with a delicate nutty aroma, rich in B vitamins and an excellent source of dietary fibre and protein. Perfect for stir-fries and quick dishes.

White Oyster

פטריית צדף לבנה

Pleurotus ostreatus

A delicate, elegant mushroom with a slightly sweet flavour that readily absorbs other flavours and works beautifully in soups, sauces and stews. Low in calories and rich in prebiotic fibre that supports digestive health.

Black Pearl Oyster

פטריית פנינה שחורה

Pleurotus ostreatus (Hybrid)

A meaty mushroom with a deep, rich earthy aroma. High in protein and dietary fibre.
